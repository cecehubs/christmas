# Christmas Motion Games

Six Christmas games you play with your hands and body in front of a webcam. A mouse also works.

1. **Fill the Glass**: catch falling champagne or hot chocolate and end with a "Cheers!"
2. **Decorate**: a gingerbread cookie, a Christmas tree or a snowman. Pinch to grab and place.
3. **Firework Drawing**: draw in the air and watch it explode. Save a photo or record a video.
4. **Glitter Me**: walk past the camera and turn into glitter.
5. **Draw in the Snow**: paint with snow while flakes pile up on it.
6. **Sledding Rescue**: sled down the hill, dodge trees and rescue Santa's presents for the village.

Switch between the **Illustrated** and **Collage** styles with the tabs at the top.

## Play

Open `index.html` in Chrome and click **Start camera**. Motion tracking uses MediaPipe and needs an internet connection the first time.

If the browser blocks the camera when you open the file directly, run this in the folder:

```
python3 -m http.server
```

Then open http://localhost:8000.

**Controls:** pinch (thumb + index finger) to click, or hold your hand still over a button. With both hands in view, hold up one finger on your left hand for a colour bar, or two fingers for line weight.
